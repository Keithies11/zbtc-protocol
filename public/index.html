Claim ZBTC on Base

Submit a wallet transaction to claim newly minted ZBTC. Rewards halve every 200,000 global claims. The 21,000,000-token cap is a maximum, not a promise that every token will be minted. This is not proof-of-work mining or a BTC price peg.

Learning only. You can lose real Base ETH to gas and ZBTC has no guaranteed value. Never use a wallet containing funds you cannot afford to lose.

Value disclosure: ZBTC is real on-chain token data with a reported Base liquidity position. Liquidity and market price can change, and there is no guaranteed cash value. Your wallet balance is not a dollar valuation.

Automated multi-address farming: the cooldown is per address, not per person. One operator can claim through several addresses and sell rewards. A longer cooldown slows each address but does not prevent this or guarantee earnings or buyers.

Proposed schedule, not automatically enforced: epoch 0: 10 minutes; epoch 1: 30 minutes; epoch 2: 60 minutes; then 120, 240, 480 minutes, doubling at each halving. The deployed contract uses one owner-adjustable cooldown for all addresses. Each change requires an owner transaction; the live value above is authoritative.

Halving is claim-count based, not date based. With one continuously claiming address, the proposal takes about 3.80 years to the first halving and 15.21 years cumulatively through the second epoch. More addresses can reach these milestones faster. These are illustrations, not launch dates or forecasts.

The current contract stops rewards at epoch 64. Integer rounding leaves 0.0000000000058 ZBTC below the cap after all positive-reward epochs; exactly 21 million is never reached under these rules.
